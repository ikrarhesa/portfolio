Selected case studies

Localizing Shopee's AR effects for Indonesia

Shipped · 2022–present · Product designer, SDK. Replaced an effects library built for another market with one made for Indonesian users, refreshed it every week, and gave sellers commercial effects with text they can edit, for both Shopee Video and Shopee Live. Videos a week using AR effects in Indonesia grew from 30 to 500 after the library refresh.

Fixing low template adoption in Malaysia: 200 to 34K weekly uses

Shipped · 2023 · Designer, SDK, Malaysia design direction. Template adoption in Malaysia was low. I reused what worked in Indonesia, researched what Malaysian users like, then designed for them. Weekly uses grew from 200 to 34,000 within three months.

Tana: making recycling visible and rewarding

Concept · 2024 · Researcher and designer. A 12-week solo concept for a recycling app in Indonesia, built from interviews, a survey and visits to local waste banks. Never launched: the work is in the research and the decisions it led to.
